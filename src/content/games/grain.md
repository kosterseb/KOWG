A small kid who lives in the dunes sets out across a world that doesn't want to be crossed. Deep sand slows you down, storms force you to dig in, and the cold nights have to be survived by the fire.

There's almost no dialogue and almost no interface. You figure things out by looking: glowing runes on old stones, a lost civilisation's cryptic language, and the Fumbrella, a living fan-umbrella that works as a shield, a board and a kite, and grows with you.
