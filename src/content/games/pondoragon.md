Snake, reimagined as a dragon's life. You hatch as a tiny youngling in a pond and grow through meadows, rivers and sky, gaining legs, wings and scales as each run goes on.

Every egg belongs to a deity. Make it to godhood and your dragon joins them; fail and the egg is lost. Between runs, unlock talents and rare eggs, and chase the big chain combos.
