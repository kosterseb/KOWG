A cosy, semi-idle guild management game. Send squads of heroes out on jobs, sort through whatever they drag home, and grow your guild hall one room at a time.

Heroes wear the armour you give them, rival guilds compete for the same work, and the guild keeps running while you're away, so you can check in from your phone.
