A top-down action game built on our own engine, with meadows, dunes, snow and water that change with the weather and the time of day.

You start as the Oracle and unlock other characters as you go, each with their own way of fighting and moving. Fights are about timing, blocking and reading your opponent. Between fights there are point-to-point races through wild biomes, arenas, and a pocket-dimension hub to call home.
